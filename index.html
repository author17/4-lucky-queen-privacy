What the app does

4 Lucky Queen is an entertainment game that uses virtual credits with no cash value. It runs entirely on your device.

None. The app does not collect, send, share or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Your virtual credit balance and settings (sound, age confirmation) are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

The game simulates gambling and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.
